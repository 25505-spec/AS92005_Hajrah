About Us

Our Mission

To raise awareness about the reality of rising sea levels and empower communities to protect their futures.

Why It Matters

Though it may not seem like it , climate change is no longer a distant threat , rising temperatures are causing ocean water to expand which raises the sea level. This results in severe flooding , loss of homes for millions of people . We believe widespread awareness is the first and most critical line of defense.
