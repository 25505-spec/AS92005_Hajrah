About Us

Our Mission

Saving Our Shores is an organisation dedicated to raising awareness about the growing issue of rising sea levels and their impact on communities , ecosystems and wildlife. Due to global temperatures rising, glaciers are melting and the ocean is expanding . This causes sea levels to rise which results in an increased amount of flooding , coastal erosion and damage to peoples homes and property. Our mission is to educate , inspire and encourage people to take action . We believe that everyone can play a part in saving our planet.

Why It Matters

Though it may not seem like it , climate change is no longer a distant threat , rising temperatures are causing ocean water to expand which raises the sea level. This results in severe flooding , loss of homes for millions of people . We believe widespread awareness is the first and most critical line of defense.
