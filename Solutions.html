Saving our shores

How can we contribute to the change?

Rising sea levels are a global challenge, but there are many ways we can reduce their impact and protect our coastlines.

Reduce carbon footprint by encouraging public transport , biking , walking etc
Reduce greenhouse gas emissions by using renewable energy, improving energy efficiency, and reducing the use of fossil fuels
Reduce plastic waste by recycling often and using sustainable products to avoid pollution
Learn about climate change , support environmental organisations and encourage others to take action too
